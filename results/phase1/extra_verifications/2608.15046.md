# Verification at source: arXiv 2608.15046 "Certifying Compressed Language Models: An Audit and a Statistical Toolkit" (Amogh Singh, Georgia Tech)

Read in full on 2026-10-11 (abstract through Appendix L). v1 only, 2026-08-15; arXiv-only, 109 pages; artifacts on Zenodo.

- Object: compressed (quantized/pruned) models vs their FP16 originals, under one harness. Audits 17 published "equivalence" claims (none states a margin, none releases per-item outputs) and 1,707 paired model-by-task cells from public per-item dumps.
- Proposes: paired TOST at a declared margin (registered 2 pp; sweeps at 1 and 3); required-item tables from paired discordance (n = ceil(((z_{1-a}+z_{1-b}) sd / m)^2)); report per-item churn beside net delta; a five-line reporting standard; "Failing to find such evidence is not evidence of equivalence" (Sec. 3.1).
- Keyword search: port 0, re-implementation 0, Harbor 0, SWE-bench 0, "overlap" only as description overlap; "parity" only as a synonym for equivalence; "harness" 16 (Sec. 7: lm_eval configuration changes move per-item correctness as much as quantization does, on one model). Its pairing rule requires identical prompt hashes, so a port that changes prompts would be excluded from its analysis, not analysed.
- Does not compute false-alarm or miss rates for any heuristic acceptance rule, never treats few-run designs, gives items-needed but not runs-needed, no defect injection.
- Verdict: narrows (confidence 0.8). Cite for TOST-at-a-declared-margin, required-n, churn and per-item release; position ports as a new application with a run-level sampling unit and task-ID pairing across harnesses.
